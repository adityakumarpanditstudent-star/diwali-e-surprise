# 🪔 Diwali Gali 3D | Interactive E-Surprise ✨

A breathtaking, full-fledged 3D interactive Diwali celebration web experience inspired by traditional Indian festival aesthetics and crafted with Three.js WebGL, real-time lighting physics, and authentic acoustics.

## ✨ Features

### 🪔 1. 3D Festive Gali Walkway & Dynamic Lighting
- **56-meter Traditional Indian Gali**: Authentic architectural facade with colorful haveli houses, ornate entrance doorways, and heritage cast-iron lamp poles with brass filigree.
- **Cascade Ignition Wave**: Watch the darkness turn into radiant gold as over 100 clay curb diyas, street poles, and dense overhead catenary fairy light strings ignite sequentially from nearest to farthest.
- **360° Drag & Scroll Controls**: Fluid pointer and touch drag to look in any direction, plus scroll to zoom and take in the ambiance.

### 🎨 2. Real-Time Interactive Rangoli Mandala
- **Mandala Floor Canvas**: A sacred 1024x1024 procedural Rangoli mandala on the street paving with intricate lotus petals, peacock feather motifs, pulli border dots, and auspicious sunburst patterns.
- **Prolonged Press-to-Paint Interaction**: Hold to dynamically color the rangoli petals with vibrant turmeric yellow, kumkum red, emerald green, and peacock blue. The greeting card fades during coloring to provide an unobstructed view.
- **Surrounding Diya Perimeter**: 8 clay diyas encircle the rangoli, each igniting as the mandala is colored.

### 🏮 3. Infinite Soaring Sky Lantern
- **Handcrafted Origami 3D Lantern**: Dual-tapered translucent body, golden bamboo rings, candle flame core, radial bloom glow halo, and dangling silk tassels.
- **Infinite Flight**: When released, the lantern continuously ascends into the starry heavens without a ceiling, drifting gracefully deeper into the night sky with natural wind sway.
- **Dynamic Camera Centering**: The camera tracks the ascending lantern in real time, keeping it perfectly centered in the frame.
- **Companion Sky Lanterns**: Background lanterns rise into the night sky alongside the main lantern.

### 🌺 4. Authentic Indian Diwali Street Decorations
- **Multi-tiered Grand Brass Chandeliers (*Jhumars*)**: Suspended overhead with dual brass filigree rings, 8 perimeter clay diyas with glowing flames, hanging bell finials, and warm radial lighting.
- **Cascading Marigold & Jasmine Floral Torans (*Genda Phool & Mogra Malas*)**: Draped in catenary curves across building facades with vertical floral drops and brass *ghungroo* bells.
- **Curbside Stone Pedestal Water Urlis**: Hammered brass bowls on carved sandstone pedestals filled with calm water, floating crimson rose petals, yellow marigolds, and floating lit clay diyas.
- **3D Paper Akash Kandils**: Traditional stellated geometric star lanterns with glowing jewel-toned inner cores and hanging crepe paper streamers swaying in the breeze.
- **Doorway Floral Rangolis**: Semicircular floral pavement carpets in kumkum red and turmeric yellow outside house entrances with twin flanking floor diyas.
- **Balcony Bunting Pennants (*Bandhanwar*)**: Saffron, magenta, teal, and gold triangular flags draped along first-floor balconies.

### 🎆 5. Rooftop Fireworks Finale
- **Panoramic Night Horizon**: Ascend to the terrace overlooking the illuminated street, temple spire, and starlit sky.
- **Interactive 2D Fireworks Particle Engine**: Tap or click anywhere in the sky to launch sparkling fireworks with realistic gravity, air resistance, and crackling particle bursts.

### 🎵 6. Authentic Soothing Indian Music Player
- **Soulful Bansuri Bamboo Flute** (Live acoustic flute & tanpura, default selected)
- **Original Gali Melodic Dhun**
- **Royal Darbar Sitar Recital** (Authentic Indian classical sitar)
- **Temple Morning Meditative Flute** (Serene peaceful harmony)

### 💌 7. Royal Greeting Card & Mithai Showcase
- **Luxury Royal Card**: Gold calligraphy typography, Hindi/English greetings, and animated royal envelope seals.
- **Royal Indian Mithai Box**: Realistic, high-resolution renders of iconic festive sweets:
  - *Kaju Katli* (Diamond cashew silver-leaf fudge)
  - *Motichoor Ladoo* (Golden saffron droplets)
  - *Gulab Jamun* (Rosewater cardamom syrup dumplings)
  - *Kesar Peda* (Royal saffron cardamom mawa fudge)

### 🔗 8. Personalization & Recipient Sharing Link
- **Sender Personalization Modal**: Customize recipient and sender names, select from curated Hindi (`हिंदी`), Hinglish, or English sample messages, and choose wishes themes.
- **View-Only Recipient Mode**: Generates a shareable URL that locks the greeting for the recipient and enables direct 1-tap WhatsApp sharing.

## 🚀 Running Locally

No build tools or bundlers required — runs directly in any modern browser!

1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/adityakumarpanditstudent-star/diwali-e-surprise.git
   cd diwali-e-surprise
   ```

2. Serve with any static HTTP server (e.g., Python):
   ```bash
   python -m http.server 3000
   ```

3. Open `http://localhost:3000` in your browser.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Three.js (r128)**: 3D scene, procedural geometry, custom lighting, shadows, and camera interpolation.
- **HTML5 Canvas**: Dynamic procedural texture rendering for Rangoli, lantern glow halos, and 2D fireworks particle simulation.
- **CSS3 / Vanilla JS**: Responsive animations, glassmorphism UI cards, modal transitions, and audio player controls.
